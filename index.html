What still needs backend services before launch.

  • Database storage: replace localStorage with Postgres, Supabase, Firebase, or Airtable.
  • Email workflows: connect SendGrid, Resend, Mailchimp, or HubSpot sequences.
  • PDF generation: use a server renderer for branded quote PDFs and signed proposal URLs.
  • CRM and calendar sync: wire exports to HubSpot/Salesforce and Google/Microsoft calendars.
  • Security: add authentication, authorization, server-side validation, CAPTCHA, and audit logging.
